100 SECRET CODES TO LEARN ANYTHING

ChatGPT learns the way you do

“Type one code and your topic. ChatGPT does the studying with you.”
Original codes by @sabrina_ramonov
Shared by Stefanie Zucker
AI STRATEGY  ·  BUILD  ·  SCALE

Pick a code, type it anywhere in your prompt, and add your topic. That is the whole trick.

For example
QUIZME — photosynthesis
  • Add your class notes, a pasted article, or the actual question for a more focused answer.
  • Stack two codes in one prompt when it helps — FEYNMAN-TECHNIQUE then QUIZME is a proven pairing.
  • The first 10 are hers, in the order from the video. The other 90 are built on the same idea.
  • Works in any chat AI — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. The code carries the teaching method, not the app.

The 10 from the video

Each one is one typed code away.
ELI5
Explain this in the simplest words possible.
80/20
Skip to the 20% of this topic that does 80% of the work.
MAKE-STICKY-NOTES
Turn the key facts into a visual sticky-note guide.
FEYNMAN-TECHNIQUE
Explain it in simple words until the gaps stand out.
MAKE-MINDMAP
Map the main ideas and how they connect.
QUIZME
One question at a time, until you actually understand.
MAKE-VISUAL-STUDYGUIDE
Build a one-page study sheet with the main points.
FIRSTPRINCIPLES
Break it down to the base ideas, then build it back up.
SOCRATES
Teach me by asking me questions, one at a time.
MAKE-SCHEMATIC
Draw a schematic of how the parts fit together.

2. Understand it deeply

Codes that force the idea into shape before you memorize anything.
ANALOGY
Explain it through something I already know well.
TEACH-BACK
I explain it, you grade me like a professor.
SIMPLIFY-THEN-DEEPEN
Plain version first, then add the depth back one layer at a time.
CONCEPT-LADDER
Build me a ladder from what I know to this hard idea.
COMPARE-CONTRAST
Explain it against the thing I always confuse it with.
MISCONCEPTION-CHECK
Tell me what people usually get wrong about this first.
SELF-EXPLAIN
Make me explain each step, then correct me.
KNOWLEDGE-TREE
Map what this rests on and where it leads.
WHY-CHAIN
Ask why five times until we hit bedrock.
INTUITION-FIRST
Build my gut feeling for this before any formulas.
EDGE-CASES
Show me where this rule breaks.

3. Study and actually remember

Codes built on how memory really works.
ACTIVE-RECALL
Close the book and interrogate me on it.
MAKE-FLASHCARDS
Make Q&A flashcards I can drop into Anki or Quizlet.
SPACED-REPETITION
Turn this into a day 1, 3, 7, 14 review schedule.
MNEMONICS
Build memory hooks for the facts I keep forgetting.
MEMORY-PALACE
Walk me through placing these facts in a mental palace.
BLURT
I write everything I remember, you mark what I missed.
LEITNER-BOX
Sort these into a five-box review system.
INTERLEAVE
Mix these three topics into one practice session.
CLOZE-TEST
Blank out the key words and make me fill them in.
RETRIEVAL-LADDER
Start with what I know, pull the rest out with questions.
SLEEP-ON-IT
What should I review tonight for tomorrow to stick?

Codes for articles, textbooks and papers you paste in.

KEYTERMS

READ-LIKE-A-PROFESSOR

EXAM-QUESTIONS

LEXILE-LADDER

ARGUMENT-MAP
Map the claims, evidence and assumptions.
STEELMAN

READING-PLAN
Split this long text into a week of focused reads.
CROSS-EXAMINE
Ask me questions about this text until I prove I read it.

NIGHT-BEFORE

MARK-BOUNDARY

6. Math and problem solving

Codes that make it show its work.

SOLVE-3-WAYS

ESTIMATE-FIRST

SANITY-CHECK

SYMBOL-TRANSLATOR

UNGUIDED

FIND-MY-ERROR

INTUITION-CHECK
Quiz my gut instinct on answers before I calculate.

GRADING-SWAP

8. Language learning

Codes for absorbing a language instead of studying it.
COMPREHENSIBLE-INPUT
Write me a story at my level, just slightly above.
SHADOWING-SCRIPT
Sixty-second script with pause marks for shadowing.
SPACED-VOC
A twenty-word set for today, with review timing.
GRAMMAR-DRILL
Targeted drills on this one grammar point.
CONVO-PRACTICE
Roleplay the conversation. Correct me after, not during.
TRANSLATE-THEN-EXPLAIN
Translate it, then explain why the grammar did that.
NATIVE-REWRITE
Rewrite what I said the way a native would.
LISTENING-LADDER
Describe what to listen to at my level this week.

Codes that make the argument hold up.

COUNTERARGUE

EVIDENCE-CHECK

EDIT-LIKE-AN-EDITOR

SOCRATIC-ESSAY

DEFINITION-DRILL
Make me define my key terms, then test the definitions.
AUDIENCE-SWAP
Rewrite this for a skeptic, then for a friend.

TEACH-MODE

STUCK-PROTOCOL
